Scan states and progress

A scan is analyzed in the background, so the user can always see where it stands. The API and the UI share one state machine.

States and stages

StateStored asWhat happens and what the user sees
uploadingclient onlyThe browser sends the photo. A real percentage and a Cancel button.
processing, validatingstatus=processing stage=validatingPOST /scans checked and stored the image and answered 202. The screen says Checking image. Long edge capped at 2048 px and re-encoded as JPEG; the model receives this sanitized JPEG.
processing, analyzingstage=analyzingA background worker runs predict() in a threadpool with a timeout. Scanning beam, eased bar, elapsed time.
processing, savingstage=savingThe prediction is resolved to a plant and disease and written. The screen says Saving result.
completedstatus=completed with a verdictThe result panels open. The verdict is disease, healthy or unknown.
failedstatus=failed with a failure_codeA reason in plain words and a Retry button that reuses the stored image.

Transitions

MoveTrigger
uploading to validatingPOST /scans accepted. Answer 202 with {id, status, stage}.
validating to analyzingThe worker starts and sets stage=analyzing.
analyzing to savingpredict() returned and the resolver matched the slugs.
saving to completedThe verdict is written together with the raw prediction (jsonb).
any processing stage to failedNotImplementedError gives ml_unavailable. Any other error or a timeout gives prediction_failed. A janitor also fails scans stuck in processing too long.
failed to analyzingPOST /scans/{id}/retry on the stored image. A scan that is not failed answers 409 scan_not_failed.
completed or failed to deletedDELETE /scans/{id}. Deleting also cancels a processing scan.

Behaviors that keep users informed

Polling

Ask, do not guess

The page polls GET /scans/{id} through the Next proxy every second, backing off to two seconds, until the scan is completed or failed. The screen never invents a state.

Waiting

Reassurance, then a way out

After about 8 s the screen adds Still analyzing, larger photos take a bit longer. After the timeout it shows a clear failure with Retry on the already uploaded image.

Leaving

Navigating away is safe

The scan keeps processing. It appears in History with a live Analyzing badge and progress, and opening it resumes the progress or shows the result. Cancel is available during upload.

Access

Works for everyone

Screen readers get a live progress value and every change is announced. Reduced motion drops the 3D effects. The same bar serves account deletion.

Rejected before a scan exists

A bad image, storage trouble or a rate limit answers the upload itself. No scan row is created.
