Database

PostgreSQL 16 with the citext and pg_trgm extensions. Twelve tables: four for accounts and tokens, five for the catalog, two for scans and one outbox. The diagram on the next page shows how they connect.

Deletion rules

RuleEffect
CascadeDeleting a user removes scans, feedback, identities and tokens, and queues the S3 keys in storage_deletion.
RestrictA plant or disease that a scan uses cannot be deleted.
PairingComposite key (disease_id, plant_id) stops a disease pairing with the wrong plant.
QuotaOver the per user cap (default 500, SCAN_QUOTA) the API returns scan_quota_exceeded.

Catalog

The catalog holds 13 plants and 27 diseases from the DAHON seed. Blueberry and Soybean exist as plants with zero diseases.

Seed extraction

The one off tool tools/extract_dahon.py turns the DAHON MySQL dump into the committed seed files. These rules decide what ends up in the catalog.

RuleEffect
Plant prefix splitThe DAHON disease table has no plant foreign key. The name Plant: Disease is split on the first : and the prefix is mapped to a plant. An unmapped prefix is a hard failure.
Alias mapPepper Bell becomes Bell Pepper, Corn (Maize) becomes Corn and Cherry (including sour) becomes Cherry.
Pseudo entries droppedThe diseases Healthy and Unknown and the Unknown plant are removed. That leaves 13 plants and 27 diseases.
Typo fixHaunglongbing is corrected.
Raspberry not seededA raspberry prediction resolves to unknown. Blueberry and Soybean are seeded as plants with zero diseases.
Sample imagesHealthy and unknown sample images go to the Next app for the verdict screens. Catalog images are resized to 1280 px and deduped by hash.
Duplicated textTreatment text that is repeated under prevention is reported for manual review, not deleted.
Outputslabels.json (the contract predict() must honor), slug_overrides.yaml and cleaning_report.md.
Loaderpython -m app.seeds runs as one transaction, is idempotent and never deletes. --check asserts 13 plants and 27 diseases.

How the catalog fits together

Every disease belongs to exactly one plant. Entries, affected species and images belong to one disease, so deleting a disease removes them. A plant or disease that a scan points to is protected by RESTRICT.

Only the sha256 hash of a refresh token is stored, so a database leak does not hand out working tokens. Leafy sends no email, so there is no verify or reset token table.
