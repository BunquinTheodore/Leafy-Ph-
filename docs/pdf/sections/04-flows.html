Guests and members

WhoPages
GuestLanding, Handbook (public), Login, Register. There are no forgot, reset or verify pages because there is no email. A signed in user who opens a guest page goes to /dashboard.
MemberDashboard, Scan, Scans (history), Scan detail, Handbook, Account. A signed out visitor on a member page goes to /login?next=.
NavigationDesktop: header with Handbook, Scan, History, Dashboard, theme toggle and user menu. Mobile: bottom bar with Home, a centered Scan button, History, Handbook and Account.
