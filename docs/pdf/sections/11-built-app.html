Other scan outcomes

No verify step

Leafy sends no email, so nothing blocks a scan. Every signed in member can scan, and there is no verify prompt or resend button.

OutcomeWhat the member can do next
Disease foundRead causes, symptoms, treatment, prevention and photos, mark the result right or wrong, scan another leaf, or open the handbook page.
Healthy or unknownFollow care tips, or retake the photo with the guide.
FailedRetry on the stored image or try another photo. With the model stub, every scan ends here with Analysis isn’t available right now.

On a phone

Phone screens use a bottom navigation with Dashboard, History, Scan, Handbook and Account, edge to edge cards and safe area insets. Each page fits one screen, and longer content moves sideways in panels.
