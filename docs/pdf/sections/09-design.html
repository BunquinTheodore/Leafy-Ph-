Design and branding

Leafy looks like a lab inside a forest: calm, precise and reassuring. Below are the real logo files, colour tokens, type rules and components. The brand, tokens, logos, splash and cursor are built.

Brand foundation

Name

Leafy

Sentence case in copy. LEAFY in Josefin Sans Light capitals for the wordmark and display titles. DAHON is credited as origin only: dahon means leaf in Filipino.

Position

A plant doctor in your pocket

Snap a leaf, understand the disease, know what to do next. Draft taglines: Know your leaf. Read the leaf. Save the plant. Plant health, understood.

Voice

Calm and precise

Short sentences, plain words, active voice. No jargon without a one line explanation, no exclamation marks.

Copy exampleWhy it works
We found signs of early blight.States the finding without alarm.
This photo is a little dark. Try daylight and hold the leaf flat.Says what happened and what to do next.
Treatment: remove affected leaves and avoid wetting the foliage.Plain action, no jargon.

One value changed from the plan

The plan started the light accent at #a86a00, which reaches only 4.20 on the light background. The built token is #955d00 (5.17), so every pair in the contrast table passes AA. The CI script tokens.contrast.test.ts guards it.

Paragraph and title rules

Rules from the plan, each shown as a good and a bad example. The bad examples are only here to show what to avoid.

Paragraph shape: measure 62ch, left aligned, never justified

Do

A paragraph keeps a measure of about 62 characters, is left aligned and uses a line height of 1.65. The last line is never a single orphan word.

Short lines and an even left edge are easy to follow.

Avoid

A paragraph that runs across the full width of the screen, is justified and has a tight line height creates uneven gaps and makes it hard to find the start of the next line.

Too wide, justified, tight line height.

Titles: one line, two at most, never three

Do
Early blight on tomato

Balanced wrap. Cards show a short display name, the detail page shows the full name in two lines.

Avoid
Early blight on tomato

A narrow box forces more than two lines. Playwright fails any h1 or h2 over two lines at 360, 768 and 1440.

Paragraph and title rules, continued

No hyphenation

Do

Plant health, understood. Words move whole to the next line.

hyphens: none everywhere, including 3D text.

Avoid

Plant health, under-
stood. A word split with a hyphen at the line end.

No &shy;, no mid word breaks. A seed lint flags them in the data.

Voice: calm, never alarmist

Do

We found signs of early blight. Remove affected leaves and keep the foliage dry.

Plain words, active voice, a next step.

Avoid

Warning!!! Your plant is in serious danger and may die.

Alarmist, exclamation marks, no next step.
